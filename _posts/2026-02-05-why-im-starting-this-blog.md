After nearly two decades in technology -- from building PHP apps in Orlando to leading engineering teams at The New York Times and now serving as CTO at an AI startup -- I've accumulated a lot of opinions about how to build great technology and great teams.

<!--more-->

This blog is where I plan to share what I've learned. Expect posts about:

**AI in practice.** Not the hype cycle, but the real work of putting AI tools into production. What works, what doesn't, and what I wish someone had told me before we trained our first internal LLM at the Times.

**Engineering leadership.** How to build teams that ship. How to hire well. How to create a culture where engineers do their best work. How to bridge the gap between technical teams and the rest of the business.

**Growth engineering.** I spent years focused on subscription growth at the NYT, helping teams that contributed to doubling the digital subscriber base and adding +$500MM in ARR. The intersection of engineering and growth strategy is underexplored.

**The startup CTO role.** What it actually means to be CTO at an early-stage company vs. a large organization. The tradeoffs, the context-switching, the need to be both strategic and hands-on.

I've spoken at SxSW, been published in WIRED, and been quoted as an expert in AdWeek -- but I've never had a consistent home for my writing. That changes now.

If any of these topics resonate, stick around.
