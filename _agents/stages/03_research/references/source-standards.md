# Source Standards

## Quality Tiers

| Tier | Type | Strength | Example |
|------|------|----------|---------|
| 1 | Author's own production data | Strongest — first-hand, verifiable | "At NYT, P90 latency dropped from 1,200ms to 340ms" |
| 2 | Named companies, named researchers, published benchmarks | Strong — attributable, checkable | "Snowflake's 2025 benchmark shows 85% accuracy" |
| 3 | Industry reports, analyst estimates | Moderate — aggregated, often methodology-opaque | "Gartner estimates 40% of enterprises will..." |
| 4 | General claims, unsourced assertions | Weak — minimise | "Most teams struggle with..." |

**Target mix:** Every post should have at least one Tier 1 or Tier 2 source for every major claim. Tier 4 claims are TTNW E1/E4 risks — each one weakens the piece.

## Citation Rules

- **Format:** APA style with footnote links. Cite naturally in prose.
- **Good:** "Snowflake's benchmarks show 85%" → footnote with full citation
- **Bad:** "According to a recent study (Smith, 2024)..."
- **Verify every source.** AI-generated citations are suspect until confirmed. If a source cannot be located, mark `[SOURCE NEEDED]` — do not substitute a plausible alternative.
- **Distinguish:** correlation vs causation, statistical significance vs effect size, percentage change vs percentage-point change.
- **Prefer specific over hypothetical.** "When Mount Sinai deployed..." beats "Imagine a hospital that..."
- **No decorative citations.** Every footnote should support a specific claim the reader needs to believe.

## What Counts as Evidence

- Production data with specifics (dates, metrics, system names)
- Published research with named authors and methodology
- Named company actions with dates
- Direct quotes from named individuals
- Specific numbers in context (with base rates)

## What Does Not Count

- "Studies show..." without naming the study
- "Experts agree..." without naming experts
- Hypothetical scenarios ("Imagine a team that...")
- Industry buzzword claims without mechanism
- Plausible-sounding citations generated by AI
