# Setup Questionnaire

Answer these to configure the workspace. Answers will generate `_config/` files.

---

## Voice

1. Who is the author? (Name, role, background)
2. What's the voice in one sentence?
3. What would you never say? What would you always say?
4. If someone read your last five posts, what adjectives would they use?
5. Provide 2-3 paragraphs you've written that sound most like you.

## Writing Rules

6. Target word count range for typical posts?
7. Opening rules (claim-first? scenario-first? question-first?)
8. Closing rules (one-sentence crystalliser? CTA? open question?)
9. Structure preferences (how do you use headers? numbered sections?)
10. What annoys you most when you read other people's technical writing?

## Banned Words

11. Provide the existing kill list (or seed one from the style guide).
12. What AI-generated phrases do you catch yourself deleting?
13. What words does your industry overuse?

## Audience

14. Who is the primary reader? (Role, seniority, what they care about)
15. What does the reader already know? What do they not?
16. What other blogs/publications does this reader follow?

## Platform

17. What Jekyll frontmatter fields are required?
18. Image path conventions?
19. Any custom plugins or formatting requirements?
