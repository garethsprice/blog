# Pre-Draft TTNW: The Lieutenant With No Radio

## F1 · Thesis Architecture — PASS
Seed (22 words): "Mission command's mechanisms transfer to autonomous agents almost intact; its accountability does not, and delegation fails in the same three ways." Arguable from both sides: the War on the Rocks author would say accountability transfers too (agents can hold intent); the rebuttal author would say the analogy fails at the root. Every planned section serves the seed.

## F3 · Mechanism Before Interpretation — PASS
The nine-mechanism table is mechanism (what armies did, what the agent equivalent is). Interpretation is confined to "what does not transfer" and "how it breaks". Draft must keep each mechanism paragraph in the order: historical fact → agent translation, and not editorialise inside the history.

## Fx1 · Intellectual Honesty — PASS
Two real, named positions from September 2026 bracket the thesis. The post sides with the rebuttal on accountability and with the original on mechanism. Risk: the post must state the rebuttal's argument in terms its author would endorse, not as a foil.

## Fx3 · Stands-Alone — FLAG
Terms needing one-clause glosses for engineers: commander's intent, CCIR, backbrief, rules of engagement / positive identification, after-action review, span of control, Auftragstaktik (name once), DoDD 3000.09, human in/on the loop. Agent-side terms (approval gate, shadow mode) can stand.

## O1 · Originality — FLAG
"Mission command for AI" exists as a genre (the War on the Rocks piece itself; several defence-blog posts on "commander's intent for LLM agents"). The novel part is the direction of travel: doctrine → civilian agent operations, not AI → military, plus the failure-mode focus and a measurable symptom per failure. The draft must lead with the failures and the metrics, or it reads as another analogy post.

## E3 · Analytical Depth — PASS
The "why" is that armies and agent operators face the same constraint (no real-time channel) and armies ran the experiment for two millennia. Deeper point: every fast channel invites re-centralisation, so a better approval UI can make a system worse.

## E4 · Contextual Richness — FLAG
Rich, but the brief's history is from memory. Several items are shaky and need verification before drafting: the Roman cohort c. 200 BC (anachronistic), Napoleon's "march to the sound of the guns" as a standing order, CALL founded "after Desert Storm", DoDD 3000.09 as origin of in/on/out-of-the-loop, and the June 2026 memorandum. Drop anything unconfirmed.
