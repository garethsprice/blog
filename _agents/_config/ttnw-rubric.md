# TTNW Rubric — Full 14-Test Scoring (v1.1)

Each test scored 0.0–1.0. Anchors at 0.0, 0.5, 1.0. Rationale required.

## Fluency (max 5.0)

| # | Test | Core Question |
|---|------|---------------|
| F1 | Thesis Architecture | Clear, specific, arguable thesis in first paragraph? Every section serves it? |
| F2 | Prose Momentum | Forward motion, varied rhythm, no filler transitions? |
| F3 | Mechanism Before Interpretation | Observation separated from inference? Speculation flagged? |
| F4 | Evidence Integration | Data/examples woven in at point of need, not dumped? |
| F5 | Closing & Resolution | Ending adds rather than summarises? No engineered zinger? Practical guides: ends on a concrete next step? |

## Flexibility (max 3.0)

| # | Test | Core Question |
|---|------|---------------|
| Fx1 | Intellectual Honesty | Strongest counterargument engaged seriously? Attributable? |
| Fx2 | Register Fluidity | Moves between technical depth and accessibility? |
| Fx3 | Stands-Alone Self-Sufficiency | Works for a cold, sceptical reader with no prior context? |

## Originality (max 2.0)

| # | Test | Core Question |
|---|------|---------------|
| O1 | Originality of Thesis | Genuinely new argument or framing? |
| O2 | Freedom from Cliche | Free of stock phrases, default framings, aphoristic couplets and coined labels? |

## Elaboration (max 4.0)

| # | Test | Core Question |
|---|------|---------------|
| E1 | Concrete Specificity | Named entities, real numbers, actual examples? |
| E2 | Source Integrity | Every citation real, traceable, verified? |
| E3 | Analytical Depth | Goes beyond "what" to "why" and "so what"? |
| E4 | Contextual Richness | Situated in a broader context that amplifies? One precedent per point, each bridged to the reader's case? |

## Anchor notes

- **E4:** 1.0 = one well-chosen precedent per point, each followed by a sentence applying it to the reader's case. Stacking several precedents that teach the same lesson scores *lower*, not higher — cap at 0.6. A precedent with no bridge sentence counts as padding.
- **F2:** staccato runs (three or more short declaratives in a row) and aphoristic couplets are momentum faults, not strengths. Cap at 0.7 if they recur.
- **Fx3:** coined terms the reader must learn count against self-sufficiency.
- **Practical guides:** F1 passes when the thesis is framed as the reader's decision. Fx3 and E1 reward a single worked example carried through every section.

## Revision Tiers

- **Tier 1 (fix first):** F1, O1 — foundation problems invalidate other scores
- **Tier 2:** F3, Fx1, E3 — intellectual architecture
- **Tier 3:** F4, E1, E2, E4, Fx3 — substance
- **Tier 4 (fix last):** F2, F5, Fx2, O2 — craft and polish

## Aggregate Interpretation

| Score | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 11.0–14.0 | Publication-grade |
| 8.0–10.9 | Strong draft — targeted revision needed |
| 5.0–7.9 | Promising but unfinished — structural revision |
| < 5.0 | Needs fundamental rethinking |

## Known AI Scoring Biases

AI scorers tend **generous on F2** (prose momentum) and **Fx2** (register fluidity). Human should calibrate these two tests. AI also tends to over-score O1 (originality) — apply the "search for your thesis online" test.
