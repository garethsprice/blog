## Writing Rules
6. **Word count:** 600–1,500 standard; up to 2,500 for extended analysis.
7. **Opening:** Claim-first. First two sentences carry the argument. No warm-up.
8. **Closing:** One-sentence crystalliser. Recontextualise or elevate. Never restate.
9. **Headers:** Claims, not labels. Reader seeing only headings should reconstruct the argument.
10. **Annoyances:** Hedge-everything writing, vague claims without data, stock phrases, writing that sounds like it came from a committee.
